# Sample PDFs for docqa-thesis-mvp

Copy the PDFs in `data/pdfs/` into your project folder at `data/pdfs/`, or unzip this file at the root of the repo.

## Suggested test questions

### sample_invoice_acme.pdf
- What is the invoice number?
- What is the total amount?
- Who is the customer?

### sample_business_expense_voucher.pdf
- What is the extension number?
- What is the total expense?
- Who is the employee?

### sample_quarterly_revenue_report.pdf
- Which region generated the highest Q2 revenue?
- What is the report date?
- What product line was the strongest?

### sample_non_disclosure_agreement.pdf
- What is the effective date of the agreement?
- What is the governing law?
- How long does the agreement remain in effect?
